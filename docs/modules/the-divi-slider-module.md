# The Divi Slider Module

!!! abstract "Quick Reference"
    **What it is:** A slideshow of slides in flexible layouts, with parallax background images and fullwidth video backgrounds.
    **Where it lives:** Click the Gray Plus icon inside a Row, then pick **Slider** from the module list (or search for it).
    **Key settings:** Content (slides), Elements (arrows and dots), Navigation, Overlay.

## Add the module

1. Open the Visual Builder; Divi adds a Section automatically.
2. Click the Green Plus icon to insert a Row.
3. Click the Gray Plus icon inside the Row and choose the Slider module.

## Use cases

- **Key content:** feature products, services, or announcements on the home page.
- **Storytelling:** walk visitors through a process or timeline step by step.
- **Testimonials:** rotate customer quotes for social proof.

## Settings

The module settings open as soon as the module is added, organized into Content, Design, and Advanced tabs.

|---|---|

| [Elements](../options-groups/elements.md) | Shows or hides navigation arrows and the bullet navigation. |
| [Link](../options-groups/link.md) | Makes the whole module clickable. |
| [Background](../options-groups/background.md) | Background styles for the module. |
| [Loop](../builder/loop-builder.md) | Turns on the Loop Builder. |

## Managing slides

1. Click the module, or its Gear icon, to open the module settings.
2. To edit a slide, click the Pencil icon next to it, then change its title, body, or design.
3. To add a slide, click **Add New Slide** in the Content tab, enter the content, then use the arrow at the top left to go back.
4. To copy a slide, click its Duplicate icon.
5. To remove a slide, click its Trash icon.
