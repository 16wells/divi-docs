# The Divi AI Agent in Divi 5

!!! abstract "Quick Reference"
    **What it is:** A chat-style command interface in the Divi 5 Visual Builder that understands plain language and performs multi-step actions on your site.
    **Requires:** Divi 5 and an active Divi AI subscription (a la carte, or included with Divi Pro). No free trial.
    **Billing:** Runs on your AI Wallet. A Divi AI subscription includes a $5 monthly balance; extra funds can be added.
    **Open it:** Click the Divi AI Agent icon in the builder sidebar, type a request, and press Enter.

## Divi AI versus the Agent

**Divi AI** generates text, images, code, and layouts inside the field or module you point it at. The **Agent** is a conversation that can inspect a whole page and carry out work across many elements: adding, editing, cloning, moving, and deleting elements, changing design settings, updating presets, configuring Canvases, and navigating the builder.

## Requirements and billing

- Divi 5 is required; the Agent does not work in Divi 4.
- Text, image, and code generation outside the Agent are covered by the Divi AI subscription. The Agent is billed through your **AI Wallet** because it sends per-request calls to AI models.
- Divi connects to models through OpenRouter. Each AI company sets its own model prices; Elegant Themes adds a small percentage for transaction and business costs.
- The $5 monthly balance resets on your subscription date and does not roll over. Purchased funds are used after it is spent. The balance is tied to the Elegant Themes account and shared across all your sites.
- Costs depend on the model chosen and the request size. Simple requests on efficient models cost little; rebuilding a full page with a top-tier model costs more.
- The wallet credit appears the first time you sign in to the Agent in the Divi 5 builder.

## What it can do

**Build layouts from reference material:** a written brief, a screenshot or mockup, a website URL, or a moodboard of images. The output is real Divi sections, rows, modules, content, and styles you keep editing normally. It makes a similar design, not an exact copy.

**Edit existing layouts:** redesign sections, rearrange modules, change colors and typography, rewrite content, adjust spacing, and use your global variables, presets, and design variables.

**Repetitive sweeps in one pass**, such as:

- adding missing alt text and improving accessibility settings
- consolidating inconsistent colors into global variables
- standardizing typography, spacing, and buttons
- improving responsive layouts
- fixing spelling, grammar, and capitalization
- refining messaging and calls to action
- finding oversized images
- reviewing custom CSS and JavaScript for unneeded code

**Site tasks beyond the page** (more limited than in-builder skills):

- Create, update, duplicate, publish, unpublish, and schedule pages and posts, and find content, links, and custom fields.
- Work with navigation menus, Theme Builder templates, Divi Library items, and supported Theme Options.

**Answer Divi questions:** it searches Divi documentation, inspects the page you are on, explains features, diagnoses problems, and can often apply the fix.

## Rules, commands, and context

| Feature | How it works |
|---|---|
| Rules | Persistent guidance (brand voice, layout conventions, which variables and presets to use). Click the Rules icon, **Add New Rule**, set a title, the rule, and when it applies, then **Save Rule**. |
| Commands | Saved prompts you trigger from the chat to repeat multi-step tasks. |
| Context | Add specific modules, pages, media, or screenshots to the conversation by selecting an element on the page. |

The Agent has more than 190 purpose-built skills for inspecting and changing Divi and WordPress, which is why it can act on your real site instead of guessing.

## Choosing a model

Open the **Model** dropdown to pick a predefined option, or choose **Configure models** to select from the available models and their reasoning settings. Divi can also pick an efficient model automatically. Use stronger models for complex work and faster, cheaper ones for simple jobs.

## Planned

The source says MCP support, which would let you connect your own API key, is planned for a future update.

## FAQ highlights

- **Existing Divi AI subscribers** get the Agent at no extra charge, including the $5 balance.
- **Other users or clients** need their own Elegant Themes account with Divi AI; the balance is not shared. The Agent cannot be removed from the role editor.
- **Privacy:** page content the Agent reads (text, layout, module settings) is sent to the AI provider you selected; each provider has its own retention policy.
- **Refunds:** the 30-day refund policy covers unused AI Wallet funds only.
- **Local or staging sites** work, but need internet access.
