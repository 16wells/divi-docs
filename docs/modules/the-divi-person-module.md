# The Divi Person Module

!!! abstract "Quick Reference"
    **What it is:** A profile card combining a photo, name, position, bio, and social media links.
    **Where it lives:** Click the Gray Plus icon inside a Row, then pick **Person** from the module list (or search for it).
    **Key settings:** Text (name, position, social URLs, body), Image.

## Add the module

1. Open the Visual Builder; Divi adds a Section automatically.
2. Click the Green Plus icon to insert a Row.
3. Click the Gray Plus icon inside the Row and choose the Person module.

## Use cases

- **Team profiles:** give your business a human face with photos, titles, and short bios.
- **Speakers and guests:** introduce event speakers with their credentials.
- **Author spotlights:** feature a contributor on blog pages.

## Settings

The module settings open as soon as the module is added, organized into Content, Design, and Advanced tabs.

| [Background](../options-groups/background.md) | Background styles for the module. |
