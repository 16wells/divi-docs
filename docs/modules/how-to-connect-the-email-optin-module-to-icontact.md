# How to Connect the Email Optin Module to iContact

!!! abstract "Quick Reference"
    **What it is:** The steps for linking the Divi 5 [Email Optin module](the-divi-email-optin-module.md) to iContact so form submissions land in your chosen list.
    **Where to set it:** Email Optin module settings → Content tab → Email Account.
    **You need:** An iContact App ID, username, and App Password, plus an iContact list.

## Get your iContact credentials

1. Log in to your iContact account.
2. Click your profile (top right) and choose **Settings and Billing**.
3. Open **iContact Integrations**.
4. Click **Enable** next to the WordPress Plugin option.
5. Copy the **Application ID (AppID)**, the **Username/Email Address**, and the **Password**.


## Connect the module

1. Open the Email Optin module's settings by clicking the module or its **Gear icon**.
2. Go to **Content tab → Email Account**.
3. Choose **iContact** from the provider list and click **Add**.
4. Fill in the fields:
    - **Account Name:** any label (for example `my-icontact`).
    - **App ID:** the Application ID from iContact.
    - **Username:** the iContact username.
    - **App Password:** the iContact password.
5. Click **Submit**.
6. Choose the iContact list you want to use.

## Gotchas

- Enabling the WordPress Plugin option in iContact is what exposes the AppID, so do that step first.
- The module stores iContact login details; use credentials you are comfortable placing in the site.
