# The Contact Form 7 Styler Module in Divi 5

!!! abstract "Quick Reference"
    **What it is:** A module that shows a form built with the Contact Form 7 plugin and lets you style it with Divi's design controls.
    **Requires:** The Contact Form 7 plugin installed and activated. Any form you create there becomes selectable in the module.
    **Division of labor:** Fields, logic, and email settings stay in Contact Form 7; the module handles only the design.

## Add the module

1. In the Visual Builder, click the green plus icon to insert a Row into the Section.
2. Click the gray plus icon inside the Row to open the module list.
3. Find **Contact Form 7 Styler** (or search for it) and click it.

## Use cases

- **Style existing forms:** apply consistent Divi styling to Contact Form 7 forms you already have, without rebuilding them as Divi contact forms.
- **Advanced logic with Divi design:** keep Contact Form 7's validation, conditional fields, and third-party integrations while matching the look of your Divi site.
- **Migrating a site to Divi:** restyle the existing forms in place instead of recreating them.
