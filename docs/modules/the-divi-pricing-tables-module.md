# The Divi Pricing Tables Module

!!! abstract "Quick Reference"
    **What it is:** A set of pricing tables, each with title, subtitle, price, currency, frequency, feature list, and a call-to-action button.
    **Where it lives:** Click the Gray Plus icon inside a Row, then pick **Pricing Tables** from the module list (or search for it).
    **Key settings:** Content (the tables), Elements (bullets), Featured table styles.

## Add the module

1. Open the Visual Builder; Divi adds a Section automatically.
2. Click the Green Plus icon to insert a Row.
3. Click the Gray Plus icon inside the Row and choose the Pricing Tables module.

## Use cases

- **Service plans:** show basic, standard, and premium tiers with a feature breakdown.
- **Special offers:** call out discounts, limited deals, or bundles.
- **Product comparison:** line options up side by side.

## Managing pricing tables

1. Click the module, or its Gear icon, to open the module settings.
2. To edit a table, click the Pencil icon next to it, then change its title, subtitle, content, or design.
3. To add a table, click **Add New Pricing Table** in the Content tab, enter the content, then use the arrow at the top left to go back.
4. To copy a table, click its Duplicate icon.
5. To remove a table, click its Trash icon.
