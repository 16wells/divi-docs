# The Divi Social Media Follow Module

!!! abstract "Quick Reference"
    **What it is:** Social icons linking to your profiles, with support for over 50 platforms.
    **Where it lives:** Click the Gray Plus icon inside a Row, then pick **Social Media Follow** from the module list (or search for it).
    **Key settings:** Content (the networks), Icon (Follow button, link target).

## Add the module

1. Open the Visual Builder; Divi adds a Section automatically.
2. Click the Green Plus icon to insert a Row.
3. Click the Gray Plus icon inside the Row and choose the Social Media Follow module.

## Use cases

- **Header or footer links:** make your profiles reachable from every page.
- **Landing pages:** encourage visitors to connect on social platforms.
- **Blog posts:** invite readers to follow you after they finish reading.

## Settings

The module settings open as soon as the module is added, organized into Content, Design, and Advanced tabs.

|---|---|

| Icon | Shows or hides the Follow button and sets the link target. |

| [Loop](../builder/loop-builder.md) | Turns on the Loop Builder. |

## Managing social networks

1. Click the module, or its Gear icon, to open the module settings.
2. To edit a network, click the Pencil icon next to it, then change the icon or its design.
3. To add one, click **Add New Social Network** in the Content tab, choose the icon, style it, then use the arrow at the top left to go back.
4. To copy a network, click its Duplicate icon.
5. To remove a network, click its Trash icon.
